title: "Skills"
description: "Skills are the things Orbis knows how to do — for you in chat, and on your behalf as scouts."
---

A skill is a packaged capability: instructions plus the tools it may use. Orbis uses skills in two ways.

## Skills in chat

The assistant draws on skills when you talk to it — searching your contacts, reading a thread, drafting a note. Type `/skills` in chat to see what is available, and manage which are on under **Settings → Skills**. Some skills need a connected account or an API key before they can be switched on; the settings page asks for it.

## Skills as scouts

A [scout](/ai/scouts) is a skill that Orbis runs for you on a schedule instead of waiting to be asked. Scout skills come from Orbis's catalogue and are switched on from the **Scouts** page, where each one shows what it watches, how often it runs and what it found.

<Note>
  Skills control what Orbis *can* do. In chat, you decide what it does turn by turn; as a scout, the skill's own instructions decide, within a spending limit and a fixed set of read-only tools.
</Note>
