title: "Tools"
description: "Tools are the individual actions behind skills — looking up a contact, reading a thread, creating a note — and the connected services that make them possible."
---

Tools are the individual functions Orbis executes when using a skill. A skill such as "email follow-ups" is composed of a few tools — search email threads, read a thread, look up a contact — and the assistant chooses among them as it works.

## Built-in tools

## Connected tools

The **Tools** page in the sidebar lists the services Orbis can connect to — Google, LinkedIn and more — and whether each is connected. In chat, `/tools` shows the same list. A skill that needs a service you have not connected will say so.

## Tools and scouts

Scouts use a fixed, narrow subset of tools — the ones their skill names — and never anything that sends email or creates a draft. What a run found, with its evidence, is on the scout's page; see [Scouts](/ai/scouts).
