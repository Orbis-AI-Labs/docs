---
title: "Boards"
description: "Boards group tasks. Every workspace has its boards from the start; you meet them when you create a task from a Signal or move a task."
---

A board is a named group of tasks. Boards exist so that work from different sources can be kept apart — the follow-ups your scouts surface, the pipeline you run by hand — while [Tasks](/tasks) shows everything together.

## Your boards

Every workspace is created with its boards; there is nothing to set up. You will see them in two places:

- **Creating a task from a Signal** — the **Create task** action on a [Signal](/signals) files the task on your default board; the arrow beside it lets you pick another.
- **Moving a task** — the **Board** picker in a task's edit drawer moves it between boards.

The **Tasks** board is the default: tasks created without choosing a board land there.

## Boards and the Tasks view

Boards do not have pages of their own. The **Tasks** view lists tasks across all boards, and its kanban groups them by status, not by board. Use the board picker on a task when you want to file it somewhere specific; otherwise the default board is fine.

<Note>
  Boards used to be the place AI agents picked up queued work. That model was retired in favor of [scouts](/ai/scouts), which watch on a schedule and hand you what they find as Signals — nothing acts on a board task except you and your team.
</Note>
