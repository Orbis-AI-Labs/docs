Tasks are the unit of work in Orbis. A task is something that needs to happen — a reply to send, a contact to research, a deal to advance. Tasks are created by you and your team, and by you from the [Signals](/signals) your scouts surface.

## Creating a task

You can create a task from several places:

- **The Tasks view** — click **New Task**
- **A contact or company profile** — click **Add Task** on the **Tasks** tab
- **A Signal** — click **Create task** on a Signal; the task is linked back to it and the Signal moves to **In progress**
- **Import** — bring tasks in from a file with **Import Tasks**

A task has:

- **Task** — a short description of what needs to be done
- **Contacts** and **Companies** — the people and companies it concerns
- **Due** — the date and time it is due
- **Status** — see below
- **Board** — which board it sits on (see [Boards](/boards))
- **Assignees** — the team members responsible
- **Visibility** — **Private** (only you) or **Organisation** (visible to your team)
- **Notes** — additional context

## Task statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Inbox** | New; needs triage |
| **In Progress** | Being worked on |
| **Review** | Waiting for someone to look it over |
| **Done** | Completed |

Update a status by dragging the task between kanban columns, or from the **Status** picker on the task.

<Note>
  You may still see a **Queued** column. It belonged to the retired agent queue, nothing puts tasks there any more, and it is being removed.
</Note>

## Task priorities

| Priority | When to use |
|---|---|
| **Low** | Nice to do, no deadline pressure |
| **Medium** | Standard |
| **High** | Important, needs attention soon |
| **Urgent** | Must be done now |

## The Tasks view

Switch between views with the toolbar:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="List">
    Every task in a table — title, status, due date, linked contacts, assignees. Filter, sort and search here, and select rows for bulk actions.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Kanban">
    Tasks as cards in status columns. Drag a card to change its status. Choose which fields cards show — contact, company, due date, type, created date — from the card settings.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Task actions

From a task or a selection of tasks:

- **Duplicate** — copies the task and resets its status to Inbox
- **Move to board** — files the task on another board
- **Update status**, **Copy**, **Export** and **Delete** — on a selection, from the bulk actions bar

## Tasks on profiles

Tasks linked to a contact or company appear on that record under the **Tasks** tab, so every pending and completed action for a relationship is in one place.

## Tasks from Signals

A task created from a Signal keeps the link: the Signal shows **View task**, and the task carries where it came from. Finishing the task is your call; the Signal stays in **In progress** until you mark it done.

## Comments and notifications

Team members can comment on tasks — see [Team Collaboration](/workspace/team-collaboration). You are notified when a task is assigned to you and when a task you are on is completed; manage these under **Settings → Notifications**.
